Only external product silhouettes informed the original native SVG; no technical drawings or internals were used. [STEYR AUG](https://www.steyr-arms.com/en/military-law-enforcement/assault-rifles/aug-a3/), [KRISS Vector external datasheet](https://store.kriss-usa.com/download/KRISS_VECTOR_G2/2022_KV_G2_SBR_DS.pdf), [RemArms product gallery](https://www.remarms.com/), [Accuracy International external chassis gallery](https://www.accuracyinternational.com/at-xc-sport). The AWM shape is an original game approximation of the older green chassis profile, not the current AT-XC design. Exact ASH-12/SVDK official product pages were unavailable; those two are clearly differentiated original game profiles, using large rear-box and long DMR forms respectively.
